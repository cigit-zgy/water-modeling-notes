cigit-zgy is intended for technical explanations, reproducible computational notes, and carefully scoped discussions of modelling methods. Its subject area includes wastewater process models, scientific machine learning, system identification, and scientific Python.

Published notes should separate source-backed statements, computational observations, and open questions. Draft material remains excluded from public listings and search indexes until it is ready for publication.

No affiliation, publication record, experimental result, or model performance claim will be inferred from missing information. When a note depends on data, code, or literature that has not been supplied, the limitation should remain visible rather than being replaced by a plausible-looking assumption.
